### Indications / Candidates
1. **Asymmetric Jaw** - One side of the jaw is larger or positioned differently than the other
2. **Jaw Misalignment** - Upper and lower jaws not meeting properly affecting bite and function
3. **Facial Imbalance** - Jaw position causing unbalanced facial proportions and appearance concerns
4. **Bite Problems** - Difficulty biting, chewing, or speaking due to jaw positioning issues

### Surgical Journey (3 Steps)
1. **Comprehensive Assessment** - Detailed facial analysis, 3D imaging, and digital planning for precise correction
2. **Surgical Correction** - Jaw osteotomy procedures to reposition and align the jaw bones
3. **Recovery & Stabilization** - Post-operative care with bite guidance and monitoring for optimal healing

### Recovery Timeline
1. **Immediate Post-Op (Days 1-14)** - Swelling, liquid diet, facial numbness around lips and chin
2. **Early Recovery (Weeks 3-8)** - Soft food diet, return to light activities, elastic band guidance
3. **Full Integration (3-12 Months)** - Bone healing completes, orthodontic finishing, final aesthetic results

### Treatment Pathways
**Non-Surgical:**
- Orthodontic treatment for mild bite corrections
- Dental appliances for temporary jaw positioning
- Monitoring and management of minor asymmetries

**Surgical:**
- Le Fort I osteotomy for upper jaw correction
- BSSO (Bilateral Sagittal Split Osteotomy) for lower jaw
- Genioplasty for chin correction
- Combined bimaxillary surgery for complete correction
