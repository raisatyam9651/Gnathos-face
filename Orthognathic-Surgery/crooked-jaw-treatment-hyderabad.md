## Symptoms/Indications
1. **Visible Jaw Asymmetry** - One side of the jaw appears larger or more prominent than the other
2. **Bite Problems** - Teeth don't align properly when the mouth is closed
3. **Facial Imbalance** - Noticeable difference between left and right sides of the face
4. **Difficulty Chewing** - Jaw asymmetry affecting normal eating and speaking
